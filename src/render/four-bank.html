Chordpad layout

Four-bank layout for Linux and Android. Pause/resume: A+B+X+Y; release all controls.

Hold the bank bumpers, or add them within @@WINDOW_MS@@ ms after pressing a thumb button, before that thumb releases. Tap and release to type; hold 300 ms to repeat. No letter uses a diagonal or two face buttons.

@@BANKS@@@@STICKS@@

Hold either stick direction before starting a stroke; L2 adds Shift. Meta = Super / Command. Stick-click shortcuts ignore held modifiers.

Without sticks: tap an unused R1+R2 pair for face modifiers, or L1+L2 for D-pad modifiers. Hold a mirrored direction above (Y/B/A/X = ↑/→/↓/←), including adjacent pairs, across keys or mouse clicks; release to exit. Repeat the tap to cancel. Holding a pair with typing keeps its layer, including R1+R2 navigation.
