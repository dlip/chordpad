The layout should make ordinary writing comfortable. A short chord for a common
letter helps, but the movement between letters matters too. We often type the
same words: the, and, that, with, this, from, have, you, your, and something new.

Keep the spaces, punctuation, and capitals. They are part of typing. Can I write
a sentence, correct a mistake, and start another line? Yes! The generator should
count those actions instead of treating every button combination as equally easy.
